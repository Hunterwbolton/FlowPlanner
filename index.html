Help & Guide
Views

Switch between Week, Day, and Month views using the toggle at the top. Week view shows your full calendar grid — Day view focuses on a single day with a task queue panel on the right.

Adding Tasks

Click any empty time slot in the calendar to create a task there. You can also use the + Add Task button in the header. Drag tasks to reschedule them. Right-click any task for edit, delete, and timer options.

Tags

Create color-coded tags in the sidebar to categorize your tasks. Assign tags when creating or editing a task. Tags show up in all views and are used in your Recap stats.

Pomodoro Timer

Right-click any task and choose Focus with Timer to start a Pomodoro session. The timer runs in a floating panel. Configure focus/break lengths in Settings → Pomodoro.

Recap

Click Recap in the sidebar to view weekly and monthly stats: time by tag, completion heatmap, and productivity trends.

Integrations

Use the Integrations button in the sidebar to connect Google Calendar. Once connected, your calendar events will auto-populate into Flow.

Shortcuts

Press ? or click Shortcuts in the sidebar to see all keyboard shortcuts. You can rebind any key by clicking the pencil icon next to it.

Themes & Settings

Open Settings to change your color theme (16 options), adjust day start/end times, configure the Pomodoro timer, and toggle sound effects and notifications.
